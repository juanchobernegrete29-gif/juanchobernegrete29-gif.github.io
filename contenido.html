The cheetah is faster than the lion.
→ It is the fastest land animal.

Jupiter is bigger than Earth.
→ It is the biggest planet.

Saturn is more beautiful than Mars.
→ It is the most beautiful planet.

Comparativo = 2 cosas + than. Superlativo = un grupo + the.

❌ more taller → ✅ taller

❌ the most fast → ✅ the fastest

❌ bigger that → ✅ bigger than

❌ more good → ✅ better
